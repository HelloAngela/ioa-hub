Interactive oral assessment (IOA) is used in many institutions, but the videos, guides and contacts are scattered. IOA Hub puts them in one public place, so that educators can find working examples and the people behind them.

The project is funded by a University of Sydney Community and Leadership grant, "Creating Space for Collective Leadership". It has 18 collaborators across Science, the Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences (FASS), the Division of Teaching and Learning (DTL) and the Learning Hub. Six of them are students.
