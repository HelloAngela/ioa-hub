## Support at the University of Sydney

These services are for University of Sydney staff and students.

### For staff

The Division of Teaching and Learning runs IOA workshops and publishes guidance.

- **Workshops:** search for "Interactive Oral Assessment" in Workday Learn to register.
- **Community of practice:** the [Interactive Oral Exchange](https://bit.ly/IOAexchange).
- **Online resources:** Teaching@Sydney.
- **Support built into your unit:** the Learning Hub can run sessions for your students. Email [learninghub.aclanguage@sydney.edu.au](mailto:learninghub.aclanguage@sydney.edu.au).

### For students

The [Learning Hub](https://learning-hub.sydney.edu.au/) helps students prepare for an IOA.

- **Workshops:** "How to Succeed in Interactive Oral Assessments" and "Improving Your Pronunciation". Both are listed on the [Learning Hub site](https://learning-hub.sydney.edu.au/).
- **One-to-one consultations:** [book a consultation](https://learning-hub.sydney.edu.au/one-to-one-consultation/), online or in person.
- **Quick Chats:** a [drop-in service](https://learning-hub.sydney.edu.au/quick-chats/) with no booking.
- **Online resource:** [Oral examinations for Honours and dissertation students](https://canvas.sydney.edu.au/courses/36842/pages/oral-examinations-for-honours-and-dissertation-students), with a Cogniti practice agent. You need a University of Sydney login.
